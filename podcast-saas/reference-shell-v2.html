Who counts as a neighbor?

Versions of the Golden Rule appear across many religious and ethical traditions. The historical variable is its reach: who receives equal standing, and whether institutions make that promise real. Germany’s record shows cumulative recognition at the macro level—and sharp reversals, exclusions and recoveries at the event level.

This is not a measured percentage of “rights” or morality. Historical events and dates are sourced. Recognition stages and Explore–Exploit scores are transparent authorial coding. The V‑Dem series is an independent expert estimate and is never merged mathematically with the author-coded frontier.

How to read the model

Cumulative recognition frontier

The gold staircase records the highest recognition stage ever reached across five equality lenses: gender, migration and citizenship, ethnic and religious minorities, sexual and gender minorities, and disability. It cannot fall by construction. It is a record of precedents—not Germany’s condition in each year.

Explore and Exploit

Explore opens a new claim, voice, category or institutional possibility. Exploit codifies, scales or enforces a rule already selected. Either can include or exclude: the 2006 Equal Treatment Act is inclusive Exploit; Nazi book burnings are exclusionary Exploit.

How the index is fixed

Recognition is coded 0–4 for each lens, then converted to a 0–100 frontier using equal weights and a historical running maximum. Events receive separate ordinal scores for Explore (0–3), Exploit (0–3) and moral direction (−2 to +2). The mechanism balance is 50 + (50/3) × (Explore − Exploit); it describes how an event operates, not whether it is good. “Exploit” here means institutionalization—not exploitation of a person.

Three current indicators — deliberately not compared

These 2025 figures use different units, definitions and reporting systems. They are context for distinct equality lenses, not ingredients of a combined “pressure” score.

Source and method audit

Controls built into the chart

  • The unit of coding is event × lens × jurisdiction; pre-1871 records name the relevant territory.
  • FRG and GDR are separate V‑Dem branches from 1949 to 1990.
  • Enactment, promulgation and effective dates are separated when they materially differ.
  • Factual confidence is kept separate from confidence in the Explore–Exploit interpretation.
  • Current indicators with incompatible units are never added together.

Remaining limitations

  • The frontier is monotonic by definition; it must not be described as continuous moral improvement.
  • V‑Dem is an expert-coded estimate of equal state protection, not lived safety or public attitudes; OWID’s historical-entity mapping also requires caution before unification.
  • The historical record is denser for laws, state institutions and recent decades than for everyday experience.
  • The five lenses overlap; they are analytical views, not mutually exclusive populations.
  • The chart does not infer that secularization caused inclusion. Religious and secular actors appear on multiple sides of the boundary.
